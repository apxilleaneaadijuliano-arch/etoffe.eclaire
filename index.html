Fabrics to Know and Love

Cotton

Soft. Breathable. Timeless

Silk

Smooth. Elegant. Refined

Polyester

Strong. Resilient. Practical

Linen

Cool. Crisp. Natural

Cotton

+

Feel — Soft and gentle against the skin, comfortable all day long

Best for — Everyday wear. Perfect for warm and tropical climates

Care — Machine wash warm with similar colors. Tumble dry on low heat

Silk

+

Feel — Smooth, lustrous, and lightweight with a natural sheen

Best for — Blouses, scarves, evening wear, and special occasions

Care — Hand wash cold or dry clean only. Never wring or twist

Polyester

+

Feel — Smooth, resilient, and naturally wrinkle-resistant

Best for — Outerwear, activewear, and travel-ready clothing

Care — Machine wash warm. Use low heat when drying — high heat damages it

Linen

+

Feel — Textured, crisp, and exceptionally breathable

Best for — Summer clothing and warm-weather elegance

Care — Wash in cold water. Wrinkles are part of its natural charm

Care Guide

✓ DO

  • Read the care label every time you wash
  • Separate light and dark items before washing
  • Air dry delicate pieces on a flat surface
  • Use gentle cycles for special fabrics

✗ DON'T

  • Assume everything is safe for the machine
  • Overcrowd or overload your washing machine
  • Use hot water on fabrics you do not know
  • Ignore the care instructions on the label
