Our Story

Étoffe Éclairé means "Enlightened Fabric" in French. We believe that understanding what you wear is the first step to loving what you own. Our mission is simple: make fabric knowledge elegant, clear, and accessible to everyone.

Fabrics to Know

☁️

Cotton

Natural Fiber

Soft, breathable, and comfortable. A wardrobe staple that gets better with every wash.

👑

Silk

Luxury Natural Fiber

Smooth, elegant, and temperature-regulating. Hand wash or dry clean only.

⚡

Polyester

Synthetic Fiber

Durable, wrinkle-resistant, and quick-drying. Great for travel and everyday wear.

🌿

Linen

Natural Fiber

Crisp, cool, and eco-friendly. Perfect for warm weather — embrace the wrinkles!

🐑

Wool

Natural Fiber

Warm, insulating, and long-lasting. Hand wash cold or dry clean only.

🌸

Rayon

Semi-Synthetic Fiber

Silky, lightweight, and affordable. Hand wash gently to avoid shrinking.

Care Guide

✓ Do

  • Read the care label first
  • Separate lights and darks
  • Wash delicates by hand
  • Air dry when possible

✗ Don't

  • Guess the water temperature
  • Overload your machine
  • Wring or twist delicates
  • Store damp clothes
