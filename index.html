Smooth, elegant, natural luxury

Strong, practical, wrinkle-resistant

Linen

Cool, crisp, effortlessly natural

Cotton

+

Feel: Soft and gentle against your skin. Comfortable all day long.

Best for: Everyday wear, t-shirts, dresses, and bedding. Perfect for warm climates.

Care: Machine wash warm with similar colors. Tumble dry on low heat. May shrink at high heat.

Silk

+

Feel: Smooth and lustrous. Beautiful natural sheen. Lightweight and luxurious.

Best for: Blouses, scarves, evening wear, and special occasions.

Care: Hand wash cold or dry clean only. Never wring or twist. Lay flat to dry away from sunlight.

Polyester

+

Feel: Smooth and durable. Naturally wrinkle-resistant. Keeps its shape well.

Best for: Jackets, activewear, travel clothing, and fabric blends.

Care: Machine wash warm. Use low heat when drying — high heat can damage the fibers.

Linen

+

Feel: Crisp and textured. Very breathable. Gets softer with every wash.

Best for: Summer clothing, shirts, dresses, and warm-weather style.

Care: Wash cold. Wrinkles are natural and beautiful — part of its charm. Hang to dry.

Care Guide

✓ DO

  • Read the care label before washing
  • Separate light and dark clothing
  • Air dry delicate items flat
  • Use gentle cycles for special fabrics

✗ DON'T

  • Assume everything is machine washable
  • Overfill your washing machine
  • Use hot water on unknown fabrics
  • Ignore the care instructions

Frequently Asked Questions

Natural fibers like cotton, wool, and linen shrink most easily in hot water and high heat dryers. Wash in cold water and air dry to keep your clothes the right size.
