Our Story

Étoffe Éclairé means "Enlightened Fabric" — we believe understanding what you wear is the first step to loving what you own. Our goal is simple: make fabric knowledge clear, practical, and easy to use for everyone.

Fabrics to Know

☁️ Cotton

Natural & Breathable

Soft, comfortable, and gets better with every wash. Perfect for everyday clothing.

👑 Silk

Luxurious & Elegant

Smooth and beautiful — treat gently. Hand wash or dry clean only.

⚡ Polyester

Durable & Practical

Wrinkle-resistant, quick-drying, and holds its shape well.

Care Tips

✓ DO

  • Read the care label first
  • Separate lights from darks
  • Wash delicates by hand
  • Air dry when possible

✗ DON'T

  • Guess the washing temperature
  • Overload your machine
  • Wring or twist delicates
  • Ignore the care label
