Luxurious & delicate — handle with care!

Feel: Smooth & shiny

Best for: Special occasions

Care: Hand wash cold or dry clean only

Keep It Lovely

Care Tips

Good habits = clothes that last longer!

✓ DO

  • Read the care label every time
  • Sort lights from darks
  • Wash delicates gently
  • Air dry when possible

✗ DON'T

  • Assume everything is machine-safe
  • Use hot water on unknown fabrics
  • Overload your washing machine
  • Ignore the care label
