+

Feel Gentle, soft against skin, gets better with every wash

Best For Daily essentials, warm weather, and year-round comfort

Care Wash warm or cold. Tumble dry low. Avoid high heat to prevent shrinking

Silk

Natural Luxury • Delicate

+

Feel Smooth, lustrous, cool to the touch — truly the queen of fabrics

Best For Blouses, scarves, formal wear, and sensitive skin

Care Hand wash cold or dry clean only. Lay flat to dry away from sunlight

⚡

Polyester

Synthetic • Long-Lasting

+

Feel Smooth, resilient, holds shape beautifully through wear and wash

Best For Outerwear, activewear, travel pieces — wrinkle-resistant and practical

Care Machine wash warm. Low heat only — high temperatures damage the fibers

🌿

Linen

Natural • Sustainable

+

Feel Textured, crisp, incredibly breathable — ages beautifully with softness

Best For Summer elegance, warm climates, effortless sophistication

Care Wash cold or warm. It wrinkles — that's part of its natural charm

Care Guide

Care with Grace

Simple habits that extend your wardrobe's life

✓

Always Do

  • Read and follow every care label
  • Separate lights from darks before washing
  • Air dry delicates flat in shade
  • Use gentle cycles for fine fabrics
  • Empty pockets before washing
✗

Never Do

  • Assume everything is machine washable
  • Overcrowd your washing machine
  • Use hot water on unknown fabrics
  • Rub stains aggressively — blot instead
  • Leave clothes crumpled while damp
