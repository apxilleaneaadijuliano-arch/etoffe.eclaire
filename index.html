Luxury Natural • Elegant

+

Best For Blouses, scarves, and special occasions

Synthetic • Durable

+
Feel Smooth, resilient, and wrinkle-resistant
Best For Outerwear, activewear, and travel ✈️

Natural • Eco-Friendly

+
Feel Textured, crisp, and exceptionally cool
Best For Summer outfits and warm weather elegance
Care Wash cold. Embrace the wrinkles — they're natural beauty
Care Guide

Care with Grace

Simple habits that keep your wardrobe beautiful for years

✓ DO

  • ✓ Read the care label every time
  • ✓ Sort lights from darks before washing
  • ✓ Air dry delicates on a flat surface
  • ✓ Use gentle cycles for special fabrics

✗ DON'T

  • ✗ Assume everything is machine-safe
  • ✗ Overcrowd your washing machine
  • ✗ Use hot water on unknown fabrics
  • ✗ Ignore the care instructions
