About Us

Enlightened Fashion

Too many students buy beautiful pieces only to ruin them after one wash. Online shopping makes it nearly impossible to feel fabric before you buy — and that uncertainty costs you money.

Étoffe Éclairé was born from a simple idea: fashion shouldn't be a guessing game. Knowledge is the most timeless accessory you can own.

Cotton

Natural Fiber • Breathable
+

Feel: Soft, gentle, and comfortable all day

Best for: Everyday wear — perfect for our tropical climate ☀️

Care: Machine wash warm. Avoid high heat drying.

👑

Silk

Luxury Natural • Elegant
+

Feel: Smooth, lustrous, and lightweight

Best for: Blouses, scarves, and special occasions

Care: Hand wash cold or dry clean only. Never wring!

⚡

Polyester

Synthetic • Durable
+

Feel: Smooth, resilient, and wrinkle-resistant

Best for: Outerwear, activewear, and travel ✈️

Care: Machine wash warm. Low heat only!

🌿

Linen

Natural • Eco-Friendly
+

Feel: Textured, crisp, and exceptionally cool

Best for: Summer outfits & warm weather

Care: Wash cold — wrinkles are part of its beauty!

Care with Grace

Simple habits that extend your wardrobe's life

✓ DO

  • ✓ Read the care label first
  • ✓ Sort lights and darks
  • ✓ Air dry delicates flat
  • ✓ Use gentle cycles

✗ DON'T

  • ✗ Assume everything is machine-safe
  • ✗ Overcrowd your washing machine
  • ✗ Use hot water on unknown fabrics
  • ✗ Ignore the care instructions
