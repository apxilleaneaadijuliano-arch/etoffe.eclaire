Fabrics to Know and Love

Cotton — Soft & Breathable
Silk — Smooth & Elegant
Polyester — Strong & Practical
Linen — Cool & Natural

Cotton

Natural Fiber

+

Feel: Soft, gentle, and comfortable against your skin

Best for: Everyday clothing, t-shirts, dresses, and bedding

Care: Machine wash warm with similar colors. Tumble dry on low heat. May shrink in high heat.

Silk

Luxury Natural Fiber

+

Feel: Smooth, lustrous, and lightweight with a beautiful natural sheen

Best for: Blouses, scarves, dresses, and special occasion wear

Care: Hand wash in cold water or dry clean only. Never wring or twist. Lay flat to dry away from sunlight.

Polyester

Synthetic Fiber

+

Feel: Smooth, durable, and naturally resistant to wrinkles

Best for: Jackets, activewear, travel clothing, and blends

Care: Machine wash warm. Use low heat when drying — high heat can damage or melt the fibers.

Linen

Natural Plant Fiber

+

Feel: Textured, crisp, and incredibly breathable — perfect for warm weather

Best for: Summer dresses, shirts, trousers, and home textiles

Care: Wash in cold or lukewarm water. It wrinkles easily — that is part of its natural charm! Hang to dry.

Care Guide

✓ DO

  • Read the care label before washing
  • Separate light and dark clothing
  • Air dry delicate items flat
  • Use gentle cycles for special fabrics

✗ DON'T

  • Assume everything is machine washable
  • Overfill your washing machine
  • Use hot water on unknown fabrics
  • Ignore the care instructions
