About Us

We believe that understanding what you wear is the first step to loving what you own. Our goal is to make fabric knowledge simple and accessible for everyone.

Fabrics to Know

Cotton

Natural fiber — soft, breathable, and comfortable. Great for everyday wear.

Silk

Luxurious natural fiber — smooth and elegant. Requires gentle hand washing.

Polyester

Synthetic fiber — durable, wrinkle-resistant, and quick-drying.

Care Tips

Do's

  • Check the care label before washing
  • Separate lights and darks
  • Wash delicates by hand

Don'ts

  • Use hot water unless safe
  • Overload your washing machine
  • Hang wet delicates to dry
