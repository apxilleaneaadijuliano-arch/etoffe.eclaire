Best for: Everyday essentials — perfect for our tropical climate ☀️

Silk

Luxury Natural
+

Feel: Smooth, lustrous, and incredibly lightweight

Best for: Blouses, scarves, and special occasions

Care: Hand wash cold or dry clean only. Never wring!

Elegance that flows like water — treat it gently.

⚡

Polyester

Synthetic Fiber
+

Feel: Durable, smooth, and naturally wrinkle-resistant

Best for: Outerwear, activewear, and travel-ready pieces ✈️

Care: Machine wash warm. Low heat only — it can melt!

Strong, resilient, and always ready to wear.

🌿

Linen

Natural Fiber
+

Feel: Textured, crisp, and exceptionally breathable

Best for: Summer outfits and warm-weather elegance

Care: Wash cold — embrace the wrinkles, they're part of its charm!

Cool, classic, and endlessly eco-friendly.

Preservation

Care with Grace

Small habits that keep your wardrobe beautiful for years

✓ DO

  • Read the care label — it knows best
  • Sort lights from darks before washing
  • Air dry delicates whenever possible
  • Use gentle cycles for special pieces

✗ DON'T

  • Assume everything is machine-safe
  • Crush delicate fabrics in overcrowded loads
  • Use hot water on unknown materials
  • Ignore the care instructions — they matter!

Everything You Need to Know

Always check the product description for "Material" or "Composition." If it's not there, message the seller — legitimate shops on Shopee and Lazada will happily tell you what something's made of!

Natural fibers like cotton, wool, and linen are most prone to shrinking — especially in hot water and high heat dryers. Washing in cold water and air-drying are your best protection!

Not at all! What matters most is: what it's made of, how it's stitched, and how well you care for it. A well-made cotton shirt can outlast a cheap designer piece. Smart shopping > expensive shopping!

Look for natural fibers — cotton, linen, and lightweight rayon. They breathe and keep you cool. Avoid heavy polyester or thick blends if you tend to get warm. Natural = breathable = happier skin!
