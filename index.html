Fabric Guide

Fabrics to Know & Love

☁️

Cotton

Natural • Breathable

+

Feel: Soft, gentle, comfortable all day

Best for: Everyday wear, tropical climates ☀️

Care: Machine wash warm, avoid high heat

👑

Silk

Luxury • Elegant

+

Feel: Smooth, lustrous, lightweight

Best for: Blouses, scarves, special occasions

Care: Hand wash cold or dry clean only

⚡

Polyester

Synthetic • Durable

+

Feel: Smooth, resilient, wrinkle-resistant

Best for: Outerwear, activewear, travel ✈️

Care: Machine wash warm, low heat only

🌿

Linen

Natural • Eco-Friendly

+

Feel: Textured, crisp, exceptionally cool

Best for: Summer outfits, warm weather elegance

Care: Wash cold — embrace the wrinkles!

Care Guide

Care with Grace

✓ DO

  • ✓ Read care labels every time
  • ✓ Sort lights from darks
  • ✓ Air dry delicates flat
  • ✓ Use gentle cycles

✗ DON'T

  • ✗ Assume everything is machine-safe
  • ✗ Overcrowd the washer
  • ✗ Use hot water on unknown fabrics
  • ✗ Ignore the instructions
