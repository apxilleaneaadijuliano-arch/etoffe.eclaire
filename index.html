About Us

Enlightened Fashion

Étoffe Éclairé means "Enlightened Fabric." We were born from a simple observation: too many people buy beautiful clothes only to ruin them because they don't know how to care for them.

Online shopping makes it impossible to touch fabric before buying — that uncertainty costs you money and good clothes. Our mission? Give everyone the knowledge to choose, care for, and love what they wear.

Fabrics to Know & Love

Click each card to learn more

☁️

Cotton

Natural Fiber • Breathable
+

Feel: Soft, gentle, and comfortable all day long

Best for: Everyday wear — perfect for our tropical climate ☀️

Care: Machine wash warm. Avoid high heat drying to prevent shrinking.

Timeless comfort that never goes out of style.

👑

Silk

Luxury Natural • Elegant
+

Feel: Smooth, lustrous, and incredibly lightweight

Best for: Blouses, scarves, and special occasions

Care: Hand wash cold or dry clean only. Never wring or twist!

Elegance that flows like water — treat it gently.

⚡

Polyester

Synthetic • Durable
+

Feel: Smooth, resilient, and naturally wrinkle-resistant

Best for: Outerwear, activewear, and travel-ready pieces ✈️

Care: Machine wash warm. Use low heat only — it can melt!

Strong, low-maintenance, and always ready to wear.

🌿

Linen

Natural • Eco-Friendly
+

Feel: Textured, crisp, and exceptionally breathable

Best for: Summer outfits and warm-weather elegance

Care: Wash cold — wrinkles are part of its natural beauty!

Cool, classic, and kind to the planet too.

Preservation

Care with Grace

Simple habits that keep your wardrobe beautiful for years

✓ DO

  • ✓ Read the care label every time
  • ✓ Sort lights from darks before washing
  • ✓ Air dry delicates on a flat surface
  • ✓ Use gentle cycles for special fabrics

✗ DON'T

  • ✗ Assume everything is machine-safe
  • ✗ Overcrowd your washing machine
  • ✗ Use hot water on unknown fabrics
  • ✗ Ignore the care instructions

Frequently Asked Questions
