Cotton

+

Feel: Soft and gentle against your skin. Comfortable all day.

Best for: Everyday wear, t-shirts, dresses. Perfect for warm climates.

Care: Machine wash warm. Tumble dry low. May shrink in high heat.

Silk

+

Feel: Smooth, lustrous, lightweight. Beautiful natural shine.

Best for: Blouses, scarves, special occasions.

Care: Hand wash cold or dry clean. Never wring. Lay flat to dry.

Polyester

+

Feel: Smooth, durable, naturally wrinkle-resistant.

Best for: Jackets, activewear, travel clothing.

Care: Machine wash warm. Use low heat when drying.

Linen

+

Feel: Textured, crisp, very breathable. Gets softer with each wash.

Best for: Summer clothing, warm weather style.

Care: Wash cold. Wrinkles are natural and beautiful. Hang to dry.

Care Guide

✓ DO

  • Read the care label before washing
  • Separate light and dark clothing
  • Air dry delicate items flat
  • Use gentle cycles for special fabrics

✗ DON'T

  • Assume everything is machine washable
  • Overfill your washing machine
  • Use hot water on unknown fabrics
  • Ignore the care instructions
