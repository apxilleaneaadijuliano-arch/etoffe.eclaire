Fabrics to Know & Love

Click each card to learn more about these everyday essentials!

☁️

Cotton

Natural Fiber • Breathable & Comfortable

Feel: Soft, gentle, gets softer with every wash

Best For: T-shirts, underwear, dresses, bed sheets

Care: Machine wash warm or cold • Tumble dry low • May shrink in hot water

👑

Silk

Luxury Natural • Elegant & Smooth

Feel: Luxuriously smooth, cool to touch, beautiful drape

Best For: Blouses, scarves, formal wear, lingerie

Care: Hand wash cold or dry clean only • Avoid wringing • Lay flat to dry

⚡

Polyester

Synthetic • Durable & Wrinkle-Resistant

Feel: Smooth, resilient, holds shape well

Best For: Activewear, outerwear, travel clothes, blends

Care: Machine wash warm • Low heat dry • Remove promptly from dryer

🌿

Linen

Natural • Eco-Friendly & Exceptionally Cool

Feel: Textured, crisp, gets softer with use • Naturally breathable

Best For: Summer outfits, shirts, dresses, warm weather

Care: Wash cold • Embrace the wrinkles — they're part of its charm!

🐑

Wool

Natural • Warm & Temperature-Regulating

Feel: Warm, slightly textured, naturally elastic

Best For: Sweaters, coats, suits, winter wear

Care: Hand wash cold or dry clean • Do NOT hang wet — reshape & lay flat

🧵

Rayon / Viscose

Semi-Synthetic • Silky & Affordable

Feel: Silky, drapes beautifully, lightweight

Best For: Dresses, blouses, flowing garments

Care: Hand wash cold or gentle cycle • Can shrink significantly in heat

Keep It Lovely

Care with Grace

A few simple habits can double the life of your clothes!

✓ DO

  • Read the care label — it knows your garment best!
  • Sort whites, lights, and darks before washing
  • Close zippers and buttons to prevent snags
  • Air dry delicates flat away from direct sun
  • Wash inside out to preserve color and print

✗ DON'T

  • Assume everything can go in the same wash cycle
  • Use hot water unless the label says it's safe
  • Overcrowd your washing machine — clothes need space!
  • Twist or wring delicate fabrics — gently squeeze instead
  • Store damp clothes — mildew ruins fabric permanently
