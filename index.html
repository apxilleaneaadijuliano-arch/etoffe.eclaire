Our Story

Étoffe Éclairé — meaning "Enlightened Fabric" — was born from a simple belief: elegance begins with understanding.

When you know your fabrics, you choose better, care easier, and build a wardrobe that truly lasts. We're here to make that knowledge as beautiful as the clothes you wear.

Cotton

Natural Fiber

Soft, timeless, and endlessly versatile.

Origin: The fluffy boll of the cotton plant

Vibe: Breathable • Hypoallergenic • Gets softer with time

Best for: Everyday essentials, tees, dresses, linens

Care: Machine wash warm or cold • Tumble dry low

👑

Silk

Luxury Natural

Nature's most elegant masterpiece.

Origin: Cocoons of the silkworm

Vibe: Luxurious • Temperature-regulating • Timeless

Best for: Blouses, scarves, evening wear, lingerie

Care: Hand wash cold or dry clean • Lay flat to dry

⚡

Polyester

Synthetic Fiber

Practical beauty that travels beautifully.

Origin: Man-made from polymer fibers

Vibe: Durable • Wrinkle-resistant • Quick-drying

Best for: Activewear, outerwear, travel-friendly pieces

Care: Machine wash • Low heat • Remove promptly

🌿

Linen

Natural Fiber

Timeless cool — naturally beautiful.

Origin: Stems of the flax plant

Vibe: Exceptionally breathable • Eco-friendly • Characteristic wrinkles

Best for: Summer outfits, shirts, dresses, warm days

Care: Wash cold • Embrace the creases — they're part of its charm!

🐑

Wool

Natural Fiber

Warmth that breathes, season after season.

Origin: Fleece of sheep and other animals

Vibe: Naturally insulating • Wrinkle-resistant • Biodegradable

Best for: Sweaters, coats, suits, winter essentials

Care: Hand wash cold or dry clean • Reshape & lay flat to dry

🌸

Rayon / Viscose

Semi-Synthetic

Silky elegance with a gentle heart.

Origin: Regenerated cellulose from wood pulp

Vibe: Silky drape • Lightweight • Breathable

Best for: Flowy dresses, blouses, feminine silhouettes

Care: Hand wash cold or gentle cycle • Can shrink — be gentle!

Care with Grace

A little love goes a very long way

✨

Always Do

  • ✓ Read the care label — it knows best
  • ✓ Sort lights from darks before washing
  • ✓ Close zippers and buttons to protect fabric
  • ✓ Wash delicates inside out in cool water
  • ✓ Air dry whenever possible — your clothes will thank you
💫

Never Do

  • ✗ Guess the washing temperature — always check
  • ✗ Overcrowd your machine — clothes need room to move
  • ✗ Twist or wring delicate fabrics — gently squeeze instead
  • ✗ Dry silk or wool in direct sunlight — it fades and weakens fibers
  • ✗ Store damp clothes — mildew is a fabric's worst enemy
