Fabrics to Know and Love

Soft and breathable

Luxurious and smooth

Polyester

Strong and wrinkle-resistant

Cool and natural

Cotton +

Feel: Soft and comfortable all day

Best for: Everyday wear, warm climates

Care: Machine wash warm. Tumble dry low.

Silk +

Feel: Smooth and lustrous

Best for: Special occasions, elegant clothing

Care: Hand wash cold or dry clean. Do not wring.

Polyester +

Feel: Smooth and resilient

Best for: Outerwear, activewear, travel

Care: Machine wash warm. Use low heat setting.

Linen +

Feel: Crisp and breathable

Best for: Summer clothing, warm weather

Care: Wash cold. Wrinkles are natural and beautiful.

Care Guide

✓ DO

  • Read the care label every time
  • Separate lights and darks before washing
  • Air dry delicate items flat
  • Use gentle cycles for special fabrics

✗ DON'T

  • Assume everything is machine washable
  • Overload your washing machine
  • Use hot water on unknown fabrics
  • Ignore the care instructions
