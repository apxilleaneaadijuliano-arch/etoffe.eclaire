Our Story

Étoffe Éclairé means "Enlightened Fabric" in French. We believe elegance begins with understanding. When you know your fabrics, you cherish your clothes longer — and that's true style.

Care with Grace

✓ Always Do

  • ✓ Read the care label — it knows best
  • ✓ Separate lights, whites, and darks
  • ✓ Wash delicates gently by hand
  • ✓ Air dry whenever you can
  • ✓ Fold or hang with love

✗ Never Do

  • ✗ Guess the water temperature
  • ✗ Stuff too many clothes in the machine
  • ✗ Wring or twist delicate fabrics
  • ✗ Leave silk & wool in the sun
  • ✗ Store anything while damp
